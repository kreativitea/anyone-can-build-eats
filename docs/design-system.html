Design system

Every part of the Gaidai Eats pages, on one page. The colours are Kansai Gaidai University's, from its own website. The places and reviews on this page are made up. Press the button at the bottom right to see the dark theme; the site itself follows the reader's device setting.

Colour

The university's navy carries the brand: the header, headings, the primary button. Its blue is for links and focus. Its orange, darkened until it passes contrast, is for stars and ratings only. Its tile colours mark the categories.

Type

One family: the device's own sans-serif, with a Japanese face named after it, so a Japanese name and an English one sit on one line at the same weight. Nothing is downloaded.
